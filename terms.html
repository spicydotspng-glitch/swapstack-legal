Swapstack Pro

Swapstack Pro is a one-time, non-consumable purchase through the App Store. It is not a subscription and does not renew. It unlocks Hard and Expert computer players, tables of 5 to 7 players and the Chaos table, the Pro decks, Speedrun, unlimited daily puzzles, table felts and card backs, and stats. When a Pro player hosts an online game, everyone at that table plays with the Pro table and decks for that game. You can restore the purchase on your other devices with Restore purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Online play

Online games run through Apple Game Center and require a Game Center account. Be kind to the people you play with. We do not run or moderate the matches and cannot guarantee that a match stays connected.
