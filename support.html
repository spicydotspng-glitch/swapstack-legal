How to play

Tap one of your stacks to pick it up. Drop one card into the middle, then take one back (you may take back the card you dropped). Four of the same item in four colours lock face up. Watch out for look-alikes: a cupcake is not a muffin. Lock all six and tap the big button. In the app: Settings, How to play.

Playing with friends

Sign in to Game Center in your device Settings. In Swapstack tap Play with friends, then Invite friends or Quick match. Up to 4 players can join a free host; a Pro host seats up to 7. If the host leaves, the next player takes over and the game goes on; a player who drops is replaced by a computer player until they come back.

Purchases

Swapstack Pro is a one-time purchase. If you bought it before, open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase.
