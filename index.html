Swap fast. Stack four. Shout first.

Swapstack is a real-time card race for iPhone and iPad. Everyone plays at the same time: lift one of your six face-down stacks, drop a card into the middle, grab one back, and lock four of the same item in four colours. Lock all six sets first and you win.

Play against computer players from Easy to Expert, or with friends online through Apple Game Center.
