Privacy Policy

Updated 10 November 2026

The short version

Swapstack does not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no account of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We run no servers.

Playing with friends

Online games use Apple Game Center. Game Center is Apple's service: your Game Center name and the moves of a match travel between the players' devices through Apple, under Apple's privacy policy. We never receive your Game Center details, your friends list or your messages.

On your device

Your settings, stats, daily puzzle streak and best times are kept on your device only and are not sent anywhere.

Purchases

Swapstack Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app asks Apple whether the purchase is active on your device.

Children

Swapstack does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. Questions: nivasayag@gmail.com.
